New Season 2026-2027 at Step Up Dance Studio Thessaloniki

5 Sep · 5 min read

The new 2026 to 2027 season has arrived at Step Up Dance Studio in Thessaloniki, and it brings three brand new classes alongside a programme already loved by hundreds of students of every age. From pole dancing and aerial to Latin dance and the very first classes designed for babies and kids, this year's lineup is the richest we've ever put together.

Whether you're thinking about starting something new this season, or you just want to see what's changed since last year, here's everything you need to know.

What's New This Season

New

Aerial Pole

A private class for very small groups, from 1 to 3 people, for anyone who wants personalised attention and faster progress on the pole. Booked by appointment, based on availability.

New

Mama & Baby

Starting in October, for mothers with babies from 3 months to 2 years old, carried in a baby wrap. A gentle way to return to movement, with your little one right there with you.

New

Acro Kids

Acrobatics for children and teens, in two age groups: 6 to 12 and 13 to 17. Balance, strength and confidence, built through play.

Alongside these, Bungee Fitness continues too, our aerial workout programme that quickly became one of our most popular classes, booked by appointment for groups of 1 to 5, with the option of private groups.

All the Classes This Season

The full dance and fitness programme in Thessaloniki for the 2026-2027 season includes:

Pole & Aerial

  • Pole Dancing
  • Aerial Pole, new, private class
  • Aerial Dance, Sling & Hoop
  • Flexibility

Latin & Performance

  • Exotic Dance
  • Salsa
  • Bachata
  • Salsa Ladies Style
  • High Heels, Commercial, all levels

Fitness

  • Bungee Fitness

Mom & Kids

  • Mama & Baby, new, starting in October
  • Acro Kids, new, ages 6 to 12 and 13 to 17

Want the full pricing and class combinations? All this season's packages and prices are on the Classes & Pricing page. The more classes you combine, the bigger the discount, so message us or stop by reception for an exact quote.

Frequently Asked Questions About the New Season

What are the new classes at Step Up Dance Studio for the 2026-2027 season?

Three classes are new this year: Aerial Pole, taught in very small groups by appointment, Mama & Baby for mothers with babies from 3 months to 2 years, and Acro Kids for children and teens.

Which classes need no previous experience?

Every class at Step Up Dance Studio has a beginner track, from Pole Dancing and Aerial to Salsa, Bachata and Bungee Fitness. You don't need any previous experience to get started.

How do I sign up for a class?

Message or call the studio, check prices on the Classes & Pricing page, and book your spot before this season's classes fill up.
