What it is

High Heels Commercial is more than a pair of heels

High Heels Dance combines contemporary commercial choreography with technique that helps you move with greater control. Work on walking, posture, balance, lines and your connection to the music.

Heels are not a requirement for joining the class. You can start in comfortable shoes, learn the foundations and add heels when you feel ready.

01

Warm-up and posture

Prepare your body and ankles, and learn the basic positions with or without heels.

02

Technique and musicality

Work on walking, weight transfers, lines and the way movement connects with music.

03

Choreography

Link the elements into a commercial routine and gradually make it your own.

What you train

Technique that supports personal expression

Balance and posture

Before you start

High Heels Dance FAQs

What is High Heels Dance?

It is a dance class combining walking and balance technique, musicality, choreography and personal expression.

Do I need previous dance experience?

No. The class is suitable for beginners, with technique and choreography taught progressively.

Do I need to wear heels in my first class?

No. You can begin without heels and add them when you feel ready.

What shoes and clothes do I need?

Wear clothes that allow you to move freely. If you use heels, choose a secure shoe that supports the ankle; the instructor can guide you before you buy a pair.
