Our studio, since 2011

More than dance classes

At Step Up Dance Studio, we believe dance is a way to discover yourself, physically and emotionally. We hosted the first Zumba Training in Greece and were among the first Pole Dance schools in Northern Greece.

A newly renovated studio with small groups and a welcoming atmosphere in the heart of Thessaloniki, at 5 Stratigou Brantouna St.
